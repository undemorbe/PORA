Pora is a shared shopping list for couples, families and flatmates.

ONE LIST FOR EVERYONE
• Create a family group and invite members with a link or QR code
• Changes sync in real time — see who added what
• Personal lists for things only you need
• Sections, quantities, urgent items and swipe actions

RECIPE → LIST IN SECONDS
• Share a recipe link from your browser or YouTube to Pora
• Pora extracts the ingredients and adds them to the list you choose
• Items you already have on the list are not duplicated

SMART REMINDERS
• Pora learns how often you buy things and suggests what will run out soon
• Set repeating reminders for items you buy regularly
• Notify family members about an urgent item

PORA AI ASSISTANT
• Ask for meal ideas, shopping tips or a list for a dinner party
• Turn an answer into a shopping list with one tap
• AI answers can contain mistakes — check important information. You can report any answer right in the chat.

ALSO
• Home screen widget and app shortcuts
• Light and dark theme, Material You colors
• English, Russian, German, French and Chinese

Your account can be deleted at any time in Settings → Delete account.
